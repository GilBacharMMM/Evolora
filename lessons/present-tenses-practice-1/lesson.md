**A Day at School**

Tom usually walks to school. Today, he is riding his bike to school. His friend Emma plays basketball every morning. Right now, she is reading a book in the classroom. Their teacher usually writes on the board. At the moment, she is talking to the students.

## [QUESTION: multiple choice] [LEVEL 1]

"My father works in a hospital."

- (✔) Present Simple
- Present Progressive

- Hint: Is there am/is/are with a verb ending in "-ing"?
- Solution: Present Simple. "Works" is one word with "-s", and working in a hospital is a regular action.
- Why it's right: Correct. "Works" is the Present Simple, for a regular action.
- Tip: [NEW: suggested by Claude] How do I recognise the Present Progressive? | It has two parts: am, is or are, and a verb ending in "-ing", like "is playing".
