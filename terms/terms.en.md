# Terms of use (DRAFT, version 0.2)

**This is a draft for the pilot. It has not been approved yet, and a lawyer will check the final text before Evolora is used beyond the pilot.**

## What Evolora is
Evolora is learning software. Your teachers use it to give you lessons, practice and exams, to check your work and to follow your progress. This year Evolora is running as a pilot at your school.

## What Evolora saves
- **Details from your Google account:** your name and email address, so Evolora knows who you are and which classes you are in.
- **Your work:** every answer and every attempt, the hints and solutions you saw, and the help tips you opened.
- **Grades and comments:** your grades, and the comments your teachers write to you.
- **Recordings:** answers you record aloud, and comments your teachers record for you.
- **Time on lessons:** how long you worked on each lesson, and how many times and for how long you were away from it. "Away" means Evolora was not the tab in front of you (for example, you switched to another tab or closed the computer), or you didn't touch the mouse, keyboard or screen for 2 minutes. Evolora **never knows what you did** while away.

## Who can see it
Only you and the teachers of your class. Other students can never see your work, grades or recordings. Teachers see only what belongs to their own class.

## How long it is kept
- Recordings are kept until the end of the school year (the end of July), then deleted.
- Everything else is kept for the length of the pilot, so your teachers can follow your progress.

## Where it is kept
On Google Cloud, in Israel. It is backed up every day.

## What Evolora doesn't do
- There is no advertising in Evolora.
- Your data is never sold, and never shared outside the school.

## When the terms change
If we add something new that Evolora saves (for example, conversations with an AI helper), the terms will be updated, and you will be asked to agree again at your next visit.

## Questions and requests
You can ask your teacher any question. If you want to see the information saved about you, or ask for it to be deleted, ask your teacher, who will pass the request on.
