# Terms of use (DRAFT, version 0.1)

**This is a draft for the pilot. It has not been approved yet, and a lawyer will check the final text.**

## What Evolora saves
Evolora saves every answer you give, every attempt, the hints and solutions you see, and the help tips you open. It also saves your grades and your teachers' comments.

## Who can see it
Only you and the teachers of your class. Other students can never see your work.

## Audio recordings
Some questions ask you to record a spoken answer, and teachers can record comments for you. Only you and your class's teachers can listen to them. Recordings are kept until the end of the school year (the end of July) and then deleted.

## Time on lessons
In a later version, Evolora will measure how long you work on each lesson, and how many times and for how long you were away from it (for example, in another tab). Evolora never knows what you did while away.

## Where your data is kept
On Google Cloud, in Israel. There is no advertising in Evolora, and your data is never sold or shared outside the school.

## Questions
Ask your teacher.
