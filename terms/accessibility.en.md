## Our commitment
We want every student and teacher, including people with disabilities, to be able to learn and teach with Evolora. We are working so that Evolora meets the Israeli standard IS 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA.

## Where we stand today
**Evolora partly meets the standard.** We are checking every screen and fixing what is missing.

Already accessible:
- All text is real text, not pictures of text. It can be enlarged and read aloud by reading tools, such as "Select to speak" on a Chromebook.
- Colours that show a status always come with a word or an icon.
- The main actions can also be done with the keyboard.
- Evolora works in Hebrew, Arabic and English, right to left and left to right.

What we are improving now:
- Colour contrast, text size, and enlarging the page to 200%.
- Full keyboard use, in a sensible order, always showing where you are.
- A name for every field and button, and error messages that reading tools read aloud.

Known limits:
- **Teachers write the lessons.** Some lessons may have a picture without a description, or an audio file without a transcript.
- **YouTube videos:** captions depend on whoever uploaded the video.
- **Maths formulas:** we have not yet checked that reading tools read them correctly.

## Using accessibility tools
Evolora has no "accessibility toolbar". Accessibility is built into the site itself and works with your computer's and browser's own tools:
- **Enlarge:** Ctrl and + (on a Mac: Cmd and +).
- **Read aloud:** "Select to speak" or ChromeVox on a Chromebook, NVDA or Narrator on Windows, VoiceOver on a Mac.
- **Keyboard:** Tab to move forward, Shift and Tab to go back, Enter to choose.

Supported browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, latest versions.

## Found a problem? Contact us
If something in Evolora is not accessible to you, we want to know and fix it.
- **Accessibility coordinator:** Gil Bachar

It helps to tell us which page the problem was on, what you tried to do, and which computer, browser and tool you used.

## Physical accessibility
Evolora is an online service only. It has no office or place that receives visitors.
